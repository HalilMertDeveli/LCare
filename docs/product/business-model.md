# Business model

**Stage 1 — centrally operated service business (current).** Submitting a service request is free.
Revenue comes from:

- paid remote technical support sessions
- on-site service visits and technician dispatch
- LED screen repair and maintenance
- replacement parts and installation
- periodic maintenance contracts

**Stage 2 — SaaS (future).** Independent LED service companies subscribe to LEDCare OS and run their own
operation as separate tenants. The data model is already tenant-aware (`Organization` + `OrganizationId` on
every business record + organization-scoped memberships), but no subscription/billing system is built yet.

Payments will use iyzico (Türkiye) and are not connected during Phase 0.
